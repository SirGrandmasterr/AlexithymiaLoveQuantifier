## What this is

You are being asked to read some short sentences out loud and have them recorded. The
sentences are written for a test; they are **not** about you and nothing in them is true of
you. They describe an invented person's day — feelings, a couple of invented friends called
Alex, Lucie and Sam, some ordinary complaints about work and a move.

The recordings are used to measure how accurately a small speech model, running on a phone
or a laptop, writes down what was said and which feelings it picks out. That is the whole
purpose.

## What happens to the recording

- It is stored on the operator's own machines and on their backup. It is **not** uploaded to
  any service, not sent to any model provider, and not published — the project's repository
  is set up to refuse the files.
- It is used to run an evaluation, possibly many times, and the resulting numbers go into a
  written report. **The report does not contain the audio and does not name you**; it refers
  to `spNN`.
- No model is trained on it.
- It is kept as long as the evaluation is being re-run, and deleted when it is not.

You can withdraw at any time, without giving a reason, by telling the operator. The
recordings are deleted, and the next report says the suite changed. Reports already written
are not rewritten — they were true when they were written.
